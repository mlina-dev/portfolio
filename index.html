Featured Project

Leopards Resort and Hotel Automated Booking System

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • PHP

Our team of 6 partnered with Leopards Resort and Hotel to develop an automated booking system designed to streamline the reservation process, reduce repetitive and time-consuming tasks, and minimize booking errors. The system aims to improve the efficiency of managing reservations while reducing the likelihood of human error, allowing the hotel staff to focus on providing better service to their guests.

Visit Site View App

Problems & Solutions

Manual Booking Process

The automated booking system streamlines the reservation process, making bookings faster and more efficient.

Human Errors

The system automates data entry and validation to minimize errors and maintain accurate reservation records.

Double Bookings

The system automatically updates room availability, helping prevent conflicting or duplicate reservations.

Disorganized Records

The system organizes reservation data in one centralized database, allowing staff to access and manage records more easily.

Inefficient Management

By automating routine tasks, the system allows staff to manage bookings more efficiently and focus on providing better service to guests.
